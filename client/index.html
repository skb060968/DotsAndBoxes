❓ How to Play

🎯 Objective

Complete more boxes than your opponents to win the game!

🎮 Gameplay

  1. Take Turns: Players take turns drawing lines between adjacent dots
  2. Complete Boxes: When you draw the 4th side of a box, you claim it and get another turn
  3. Keep Playing: Continue until all boxes are completed
  4. Win: The player with the most boxes wins!

🎨 Grid Layout

The game uses an 11×6 grid of dots, creating 50 boxes to compete for. Each box you complete adds to your score!

👥 Players

Play with 2-4 players. The active player's card will glow green with a "TURN" badge.

💡 Strategy Tip

Try to avoid giving your opponent the chance to complete boxes. Sometimes it's better to draw lines that don't complete boxes!
